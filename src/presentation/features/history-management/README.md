# History Management Feature

This feature handles the management of previously generated TTS items, including display, playback, and organization.

## Components

- **HistoryPanelComponent**: Display and management of history items
- **HistoryItemComponent**: Individual history item display and controls

## Services

- **HistoryInteractionManagerService**: Manages user interactions with history items

## Responsibilities

- History item display and organization
- History item selection
- Audio playback from history
- History item metadata management
- Search and filtering of history items
- Storage space management
